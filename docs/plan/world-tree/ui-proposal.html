World Tree: wake the forest together

A cooperative game for 9 leaders. The forest is asleep and face down. Together the team explores it, grows a Big Tree in every value's sector, harvests fruit while telling stories, finds three treasures, and gathers on the World Tree. Sun dries you out, dead leaves drift toward the centre, and if everyone runs out of water, the forest sleeps on.

Goal 2🍎 × 9 · 💎 × 3Every player has placed a fruit on the World Tree, and all three treasures are there
Goal 3🧍 × 9Everyone stands on the World Tree

How to play World Tree

El's concept, written out as one set of rules. default marks a rule the concept didn't say; each one is a decision on the Overview you can change.

Setup

  • 61 hexes in 4 rings around the World Tree. Six lines from the centre split the forest into six sectors, one per ZERAOS value (10 hexes each).
  • The World Tree is face up. The other 60 are face down: 3 treasures, hidden only in Rings 1–2; 4 springs that never run dry; 53 empty default counts.
  • Each sector draws its weather.
  • In turn, each player chooses the value they stand for, places their pawn on Ring 4 of that sector, and says why.
  • Everyone starts with 4 water (5 at most). The Keeper gives each player Enneagram types; the Secret Owl loop is dealt.

Your turn: up to 2 actions

MoveTo a hex next to you that isn't sealed. Not at 0 water. Stepping into a deeper ring for the first time draws that ring's card.
ExploreFlip the face-down hex you stand on. 2 actions in fog.
SowSeed the empty explored hex you stand on.
WaterSeeded → Grass, Grass → Shrub. Uses 1 water default.
TendShrub → Big Tree.
ClearRemove 1 layer of dead leaves from your hex or one next to you. 2 actions in rain.
HarvestTake 1 fruit from the Big Tree you stand on (carry 2 at most); tell a story about that sector's value. Not in rain. Once per tree between Tides.
Take treasureFrom a revealed treasure hex (carry 1 at most).
DrinkAt a revealed spring: back to 5 water. Springs never run dry; they are the only way to get water back.

Free, any time: pass water, fruit or treasure to someone on your hex. On the World Tree, place fruit (answer a Heartwood question) and treasures.

Weather (per sector)

☀️ SunEverything works. At the Tide, players here who aren't on a Big Tree lose 1 water.
🌧️ RainAt the Tide, Seeded and Grass grow one stage by themselves. No harvest. Clearing costs 2 actions. Nobody loses water.
🌫️ FogExplore costs 2 actions. The Enthusiast can't take their double step.

🌬️ Forest Tide (after every round)

  1. Each sector draws new weather.
  2. Rain grows plants (it doesn't refill anyone); sun dries players who aren't on a Big Tree.
  3. Forest Breath: 2 hexes at first, +1 every 2 Tides (6 at most) default. Each drawn hex blows a layer of dead leaves one step toward the centre. A plant that gets leaves drops a stage (a Big Tree doesn't) default. Two layers seal a hex.

Roles (Enneagram, always on)

The end

🌳 The forest wakes the moment all three goals are met. Then the Secret Owl guesses, the tribute chain, and trust. 💧 If every player is at 0 water, the forest sleeps on.

What the numbers say for El's settings

  1. A real challenge: the bots wake the forest in about half the games (53%), run dry in 28%, and 20% are still going at round 40. Real teams that plan together should do better than the bots.
  2. Still long: about 30 rounds. All six Big Trees by round 7, every fruit placed by round 17, the third treasure by round 25, then everyone walks to the World Tree. Most actions are moves (1 hex each).
  3. The talking: 9 whys, 27 ring cards, ~15 harvest stories, ~10 Heartwood questions: about 60 shares, roughly 90 minutes on their own. In total ≈ 4 hours at the assumed pace.
  4. If it runs too long in a playtest, the strongest lever is 3 actions a turn (about 16 rounds with treasures in Rings 1–2). Forest Breath settings barely change the result.
