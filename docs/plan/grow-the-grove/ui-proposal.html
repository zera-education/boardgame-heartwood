Grow the Grove: a board where where you stand matters

Today the map is only a clock: the season opens the next ring and sets how deep your question goes, so everyone walks Seed → Sapling → Oak and nobody's position matters to anyone else. In Grow the Grove the clearing starts bare. Every story the team tells plants or grows a tree, the trees make the Shade, and the Shade is where the vulnerable stories happen. When the grove reaches the Heartwood, it wakes.

How to play Grow the Grove

An empty clearing. In the middle sleeps the Heartwood. Around it are six Roots, one for each ZERAOS value. Grow a grove with your stories until it reaches the Roots and the Heartwood wakes.

🌱 Start: plant your seed

In turn, each player says which value they stand for and why, and stands on an empty space at the forest edge of that value's wedge. A sapling grows there: the first shade.

🚶 Walk

Up to 3 steps, each onto an empty space next to you. Nobody shares a space and you can't walk through people. Trees don't block. The Heartwood is closed until it wakes.

✦ Discover

Stop on a face-down discovery and turn it over.

☀️ / 🌑 Tell your story: the space decides

You stand onYou may tellThen
☀️ Open grounda Sun story: the bright side of this valueA 🌱 sapling takes root here, if this is the forest edge or it's next to a tree. You're its gardener.
🌱 / 🌳 Under a tree, with a Neighbour next to youa Shade story: the side you don't usually show (or Sun)A sapling grows into an 🌳 oak and drops a seed one step closer to the Heartwood.
Under a tree, alonea Sun story onlyNo one goes into the Shade alone.

You can always ask for the lighter question, or pass.

🏮 Neighbours and the lantern

Your Neighbours are the players on the six spaces touching yours. After a Shade story, one Neighbour holds the lantern: they ask you one follow-up question, and you both get +1.

Being someone's Neighbour also lets you ally with them at Dusk (you must have crossed paths this season).

💛 The end

The Heartwood wakes when the Root of every value someone at the table stands for is an oak. From then on, alliance members can step into the Heartwood to tell a Heartwood story (4 points).

After season 3 the game ends either way: alliance stories, Secret Owl guesses, the tribute chain, scores. If the Heartwood woke, everyone gets +3.

  1. Getting stuck (no empty space to walk to) happened in at most 0.3% of turns, even with 10 players: one player per space blocks a little, not a lot.
  2. Weak spot: 6–8 players at 3 turns each wake the Heartwood only about a third to half of the time. Options: 4 turns each for 6–8 players, or keep it hard on purpose.
