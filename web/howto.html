1What this is

Heartwood is a board game about getting to know each other. You play together in one room, looking at each other and at the big screen. Your phone stays mostly face down.

🗣️ Say it, and the Keeper taps it

Everything public happens on the big screen. You say what you want ("I'll step into Courage", "a lighter question, please", "I use Momentum") and the Keeper taps it.

Your phone has only four jobs: it shows your Secret Owl, it lets you give trust after someone shares, you choose your Dusk partner on it, and you guess your own Secret Owl at the end. It also reminds you of your powers.

ZERA's story is a tree's story. The seed was planted in 2021. It sprouted in 2023 and grew into a tree in 2024. The vision is a forest by 2035. (Zera is Hebrew for seed, and our mascot is an acorn. In this game an acorn 🌰 always means one thing: trust you give each other.)

  1. 🌱2021Planting the SeedSeason 1
  2. 🌿2023The SproutSeason 2
  3. 🌳2024Growing into a TreeSeason 3
  4. 🌲2035The Vision of a ForestThe finale

You are an expedition into that forest of 2035. You start at its sunny edges and walk inward, toward the Heartwood at its centre: the dense core of a tree, its strongest part. Every step inward is a deeper story.

Two goals that are really one. The real goal is to know each other more deeply. The game goal is to score the most points. The game is built so they are the same: every point comes from sharing, listening or bonding.

🧭 You, the players

Up to 10 school leaders. Each of you plays on your own phone.

  1. Open the game on your phone with the 4-letter code shown on the big screen.
  2. Type your name and pick a colour.
  3. Tap Join the expedition. The Keeper gives you your Enneagram powers, then starts the game.

🎙️ The Keeper

The facilitator, outside the game. The Keeper runs the big screen and the clock, gives each player 1 to 3 Enneagram powers, taps what players say (moves, the share timer, powers), starts each season, sets the Dusk pairs and leads the finale.

The Keeper may pause a share at any time, and can skip a turn.

2How you win

When the forest is grown, the app adds up four kinds of points. The most points wins. Nobody sees the totals until the end.

🌰 Trust

The trust acorns others gave you after your shares. For each person who gave you any: count their acorns (2 at most), then add 1 for that person.

Amir🌰1 + 1 = 2 Bea🌰🌰2 + 1 = 3 Chen🌰🌰🌰2 + 1 = 3

Trust from many people beats a lot of trust from a few.

🌳 Growth

  • Every card you kept: 🌱 Seed 1 · 🌿 Sapling 2 · 🌳 Oak 3 · 💛 Heartwood 4
  • +1 for each different value you shared in. (Heartwood cards have no value.)
  • +1 for each ✨ bonus point.

🤝 Alliances

3 points for each ally you made at Dusk. You can make up to 3.

🦉 Secret Owl

  • +1 for each thing you discovered about the colleague you watched that they confirm is true (up to 3).
  • +2 if they fail to guess you were their Secret Owl.
  • +2 if you correctly guess your own Secret Owl.

A tie? The player with more Trust points wins.

🌳 Optional: the Forest Goal

The Keeper can switch this on before the game starts. Then there is only a winner if, by the end, every one of the six values has at least one Oak story: a card answered at the 🌳 Oak tier on one of that value's spaces.

If any value is missing, the forest does not take root and nobody wins. Heartwood stories don't count for any value. So in Season 3, talk openly about who will take the hard road, and where.

3The map

The forest has 37 spaces: the Heartwood in the centre and three rings around it. It is cut into six wedges, one for each of ZERA's values.

  • 🔍Curiositybottom left
  • 🎨Creativitybottom right
  • 🤝Collaborationright
  • 🦁Couragetop
  • 💗Compassiontop left
  • ⚓Commitmentleft

The first three describe ZERA's learning environment; the last three, the leaders ZERA forms. Each wedge has 3 Seedlands spaces, 2 on the Sapling Path and 1 in the Oak Circle.

Where you stand decides your question. The value of your space picks the deck. Your ring picks how deep the question goes.

5A turn

Each season, everyone takes one turn, in order. A turn takes about two minutes. When it's yours, look up: say what you'd like, and the Keeper taps it on the big screen. Your phone stays down.

  1. 🥾Move
  2. →
  3. ✦Discover
  4. →
  5. 💬Share
  6. →
  7. 🌰Trust

  1. 1Move optional

    Say where you want to go, or point at the big screen. The Keeper moves your pawn one neighbouring space at a time. Each step costs 1 ☀, plus the ring's extra cost when you step inward. Take as many steps as your ☀ allows, or none at all.

    On the big screen

    "One more, into Compassion." The Keeper taps the highlighted space.

  2. 2Discover

    Say "Stop here." The Keeper taps Stop here and discover. If the discovery on your space is still face down, it flips and it's yours. After that you can't move again this turn, unless you found a Hidden path.

    On the big screen
    Stop here and discover
    Spring: +2 💧

  3. 3Share

    Your question appears on the big screen, from your space's value at your ring's tier. When you start talking, the Keeper starts the timer: 45 s, 60 s or 90 s. When you finish, the Keeper stops it.

    On the big screen
    🌿 Sapling · Courage
    Tell us about a time you were afraid and did it anyway.

    Passing is always free: just say so.

  4. 4Trust

    Everyone else may pick up their phone and tap Give trust 🌰 if your share moved them. Someone with a Squirrel raises a hand to ask a follow-up. When the room is ready, the Keeper moves on to the next player.

    You keep your card for Growth points, and a 🌳 grows on your space on the big board.

    On a listener's phone
    Give trust to Amir 🌰

    Trust is a gift, not a grade. 7 left.

🍃 Passing is always free

Don't want the question? Before you start, just say:

  • Draw again: a new card from the same deck. Once per turn.
  • Lighter tier: answer the lighter question on the same card (Oak → Sapling → Seed). It scores at the lighter tier. Not in the Heartwood.
  • Pass this turn: say "pass". You lose nothing you have; you just don't keep a card this time. No questions asked.

👂 While someone else shares

  • Listen to understand. No advice, no fixing, no debating.
  • If it moved you, give trust. It's a gift, not a grade.
  • Keep quietly watching the colleague you're the Secret Owl for (see the finale).

🌙 Dusk

At the end of every season, everyone pairs up for a 4-minute conversation, all at the same time.

  1. On your phone, choose a partner you haven't paired with before, or choose Anyone (the Keeper pairs me).
  2. To make it an alliance, tick Make it an alliance. Then tap Send my choice.
  3. The Keeper taps Set the pairs. You pair only if you both chose each other. The app pairs everyone else, avoiding repeat partners where it can.
  4. Your phone shows your partner and a Bond card for this season. Talk for 4 minutes.

Alliance or Fireside chat?

Did you both choose each other?
No: the app pairs you with someone. Fireside chat, +1 💧 each.
↓ yes
Did you both tick "Make it an alliance"?
No: you still talk together. Fireside chat, +1 💧 each.
↓ yes
Are you on the same or a neighbouring space, with at least 1 💧 each? (Common Ground skips this check.)
No: you still talk together. Fireside chat, +1 💧 each.
↓ yes
🤝 Alliance! You each pay 1 💧 (nothing with Common Ground) and each gain an ally.

If you asked for an alliance and it didn't form, your phone tells you why.

🤝 What an alliance gives you

  • +3 points each at the end.
  • The key to the Heartwood: you can't step in without at least one ally.
  • An ally to jump to with Rope Team (type 6).
  • One partner per Dusk means at most one new ally per Dusk, so 3 at most.

🔥 Fireside chat

Every pair that isn't an alliance has a Fireside chat, and each person takes +1 💧. That water helps you afford an alliance next time.

With an odd number of players, one group is a trio.

Tip. Where you end your turn decides who you can ally with at Dusk. Players who move later can see where others stopped. The start player moves on each season, so that advantage goes around.

8Enneagram powers

The Enneagram describes nine personality types, each with its own gift. Before the game starts, the Keeper gives each player 1 to 3 types, and you get those types' powers. Each one works once in the whole game.

Your phone lists your powers as a reminder. At the right moment, say it aloud ("I use Momentum"): the Keeper activates it on the big screen, and it shows as used. The trail log shows who used what. "While moving" means on your turn, before you stop and discover.

  • 1Reformer
    gift: integrity

    🧭True North

    When: your turn, before you start sharing

    Look at 3 cards from your region and choose the one to answer. +1 bonus point.

    Not in the Heartwood. The bonus point arrives at the end of your turn.

  • 2Helper
    gift: care

    🤲Open Hands

    When: your turn

    Give any player 2 ☀ or 2 💧 from the bank. You both take 1 bonus point.

    Say who gets it, and ☀ or 💧. It comes from the bank, not from you.

  • 3Achiever
    gift: drive

    🚀Momentum

    When: your turn, while moving

    Take 2 ☀ and 1 💧.

    Use it before you stop, then keep walking.

  • 4Individualist
    gift: depth

    🌊Deep Water

    When: your turn, before you start sharing

    Answer the question one tier deeper than your ring (up to Oak). It still scores at your ring's tier.

    The deeper question is the reward. Not on an Oak or Heartwood question. On the Sapling Path it gives an Oak story: that counts for the Forest Goal, though the card scores 2.

  • 5Investigator
    gift: insight

    🔎Field Notes

    When: your turn, while moving

    See every hidden discovery next to you, then claim one without moving there.

    Only works if a face-down discovery is next to you. You can still move and discover your own space afterwards.

  • 6Loyalist
    gift: loyalty

    🪢Rope Team

    When: your turn, while moving

    Move to an ally's space for free, from anywhere. You both take 1 bonus point.

    You need an ally, and their ring must be open. You can keep moving afterwards.

  • 7Enthusiast
    gift: joy

    🎒Adventure

    When: your turn, while moving

    Your next 3 steps cost nothing (ring rules still apply). +1 bonus point.

    Free means no ☀ and no 💧, even into the Oak Circle. Closed rings and the Heartwood's ally rule still apply.

  • 8Challenger
    gift: strength

    🛡️Champion

    When: after someone else's share

    Give that player 2 trust acorns at once, and take 1 bonus point.

    Use it instead of Give trust for that share. Both acorns come from your own 10.

  • 9Peacemaker
    gift: harmony

    🕊️Common Ground

    When: at Dusk, before pairs are set

    Your alliance tonight works at any distance and costs neither of you water.

    You still both need to choose each other and tick alliance. If no alliance forms, the power is still spent.

9The finale: Secret Owl

At the start, your phone gives you one colleague's name: you are their Secret Owl. Tap to reveal it, and keep it to yourself. An owl watches quietly and misses nothing: all game, you observe them, while someone else quietly observes you. All the names form one loop around the room.

  1. All game: observe them. Something they love, something that shaped them, something they hope for. Listen to their shares; Dusk, Squirrels and alliances all help. Watch without being obvious, or they'll guess you're their Owl.
  2. Guess. After the last Dusk, everyone picks who they think was their Secret Owl and taps Lock in my guess. You can change it until the tributes start.
  3. Give your tribute. One at a time, around the loop, give the colleague you watched one minute:
    "Three things I discovered about you today, and one thing I appreciate."
    Then hand them an acorn: your trust. 🌰
  4. Confirm. They say how many of your three discoveries are true (0, 1, 2 or 3), and the Keeper taps it. Then they honour the colleague they watched, and so on until the loop closes.
  5. Scores. The big screen shows everyone's points and the winner. 🏆

10If your phone drops out

The small badge in the corner says ● Live while your phone is connected. If it says ● Reconnecting… or ● Offline, it catches up by itself once the Wi-Fi is back. Nothing you did is lost.

Closed the browser? Open the game again on the same phone and tap Continue as your name. On a different phone or browser, ask the Keeper for a 4-digit rejoin code and enter it under Rejoin with a code.

11The Forest Pact

The Keeper reads this at the start. It holds for the whole game, and after it.

  1. 🌲What's shared in the forest stays in the forest.
  2. 🍃Passing is always free.
  3. 👂Listen to understand: no advice, no fixing, no debating.
  4. 🌰Trust is a gift, not a grade.
  5. 📵Phones down. Pick yours up only to give trust, at Dusk, and to guess.

The Keeper may pause any share.
